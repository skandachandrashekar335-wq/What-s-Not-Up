# Privacy Policy — What's Not Up

**The extension does not collect, store, or transmit content. Privacy transformations are applied locally to content already rendered by the website.**

The full privacy model — permissions, what is stored, what is not, and how local processing works — is documented in:

**[`docs/privacy.md`](docs/privacy.md)**

Summary:

- No backend, no telemetry, no analytics
- No message storage, no message transmission
- No credential access, no network interception
- No WhatsApp or Instagram API usage
- Only your settings are stored, in `chrome.storage.local`
- Minimal permissions: `storage`, `tabs`, and two specific host origins
